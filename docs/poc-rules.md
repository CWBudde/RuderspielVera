# Ruderspiel – PoC rules

The family's rules draft (`docs/ruderspiel-regelwerk.md`) leaves many points **Offen**. To make a
first playable computer version, this document fixes every missing rule. **Every rule below is a
PoC assumption** unless it quotes the Regelwerk directly; all of them can be changed by the family.
Numbers live in `internal/data/*.json`, not in code, so most changes are data edits:

| File                                              | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rules.json`                                      | training points, camp, selections, incident probabilities, Krebs penalty, weather table, event cards per station, deal sizes, draft, medal points, Siegesmotivation, loan boat value, JTFO variants, boat/trainer scaling, guest crews |
| `calendar.json`                                   | the 52 stations                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `competitions.json`                               | weights, race lists and guest-crew offset per competition                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `rowers.json`                                     | 55 rower cards (generated by `cmd/gendata`, hand-editable)                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `boats.json`, `trainers.json`                     | boat and trainer cards                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `events.json`, `ereignisse.json`, `missions.json` | event deck, Ereignis deck, mission catalog                                                                                                                                                                                             |

## 1. Season and calendar

- PoC assumption: exactly the 12 sections of Regelwerk §2.2, 52 stations, played in order. The game
  ends after the RVB-Endspurtregatta.
- PoC assumption: **Hankensbüttel** and **Kassel** (the station before the Kasselregatta) are
  _Trainingslager_ (camps).
- PoC assumption: **WR5**, **Basketballturnier** and **Wanderfahrt** are _selection_ stations
  (choose 5 rowers).
- Mindoor-Cup, DRC-Ergo-Cup and Osnabrücker Ergo-Cup are Ergo-Cups; JTFO is a team competition;
  Hildesheim, Bremen, Kassel, Verbandsregatta I/II, Schillerschule, RVH and RVB are water regattas.
- Counts: 12 trainings, 2 camps, 3 selections, 11 Event stations, 11 Ereignis stations, 12
  competitions.

## 2. Cards and starting deal

### 2.1 Rowers (Ruderer)

- 55 cards, names exactly from Regelwerk §14.1–14.3.
- Rank (= sum of the five starting stats incl. Motivation, §5.3) per card from the handwritten list in
  `docs/ruderspiel-basiswerte.md`: every value 46…100 once; A-Junioren 84…100 (Michel = 100),
  B-Junioren 58…93, Kinder 46…59 (the age ranges overlap).
- PoC assumption: each sum is split by one of five archetypes with ±15 % jitter per stat, every stat
  ≥ 5:

  | -------------- | ----: | -------: | ------: | ------: | ---------: |
  | Kraftpaket     |  30 % |     20 % |    18 % |    16 % |       16 % |
  | Ausdauerläufer |  18 % |     30 % |    16 % |    18 % |       18 % |
  | Sprinter       |  20 % |     15 % |    30 % |    19 % |       16 % |
  | Techniker      |  16 % |     18 % |    19 % |    30 % |       17 % |
  | Kämpferherz    |  19 % |     19 % |    17 % |    17 % |       28 % |

  `go run ./cmd/gendata` regenerates `rowers.json` deterministically (seed 2026). Values may be

- IDs are `<name>-<age>` (e.g. `lea-b`, `lea-a`); the second Matilda among the A-Junioren is
  `matilda-a-2` with the display name "Matilda (2)".
- Stats have no maximum (§5.2) and never drop below 0.

- Designer rule: every player owns the eight **Kraftwerk** (value 8), rowed only at the
  RVB-Endspurt. Human players (not bots) are offered the eight **Paintrain** (value 12) in the last
  draft before the RVB-Endspurt, as an extra card only for them; taking it costs that draft pick.

- Designer rule: a **Leihboot** (loan boat, JTFO and Hildesheim) has value 0.

### 2.4.1 Trainer mood (Laune)

Designer rule, PoC numbers (`rules.json` `trainerMood`). Every trainer has a public mood, a bounded
random walk:

- It starts at 0 for all ten trainers (also those still in the draft pool).
- Whenever the calendar figure enters a new section (sections 2–12, i.e. 11 times per season; the
  mood is fixed for the whole section, so it is known before the section's competition), every
  trainer draws one step from −2/−1/0/+1/+2. The weights depend on the current mood, which gives a
  mild pull back to 0, and the result is clamped to [−2, +2]:

  | Mood now |  −2 |  −1 |   0 |  +1 |  +2 |
  | -------- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
  | −2       |   0 |   0 |   6 |   6 |   1 |
  | −1       |   0 |   4 |   8 |   6 |   1 |
  | 0        |   1 |   5 |   8 |   5 |   1 |
  | +1       |   1 |   6 |   8 |   4 |   0 |
  | +2       |   1 |   6 |   6 |   0 |   0 |

- The mood is added to the trainer's per-seat effect, so it counts like an effect: once per seat
  (a 4x with a +2 trainer gets +8, an ergo start +2), whether or not one of the trainer's weather or
  context effects applies.
- The cards show the mood and an arrow for the change since the previous section; the bots include
  it in their trainer choice.

## 3. Training and special stations

- Training (§9.1): exactly 10 points per player, freely on own rowers and on Kraft, Ausdauer,
  Geschwindigkeit and Technik.
- Trainingslager: exactly 30 points on **at most 5** rowers, free split (PoC assumption), same four
  stats.
- **Motivation cannot be trained** (designer rule), neither at training nor in a camp; the engine
  rejects it. Motivation comes only from selections (WR5, Basketball, Wanderfahrt), the temporary
  Siegesmotivation (4.1), events (Motivations-Event, Phönix, Verdopplung, Seelenzwilling, Livestream-Liebling,
  Maschseelauf, mission rewards; Nachtschicht im Kraftraum and Liebeskummer cost Motivation) and
  Ereignisse.
- WR5 and Basketballturnier (§9.3/9.4): choose exactly 5 rowers, each +10 Motivation.
- Wanderfahrt: choose exactly 5 Junioren (A or B; Kinder may not take part), each +4 Ausdauer and
  +3 Motivation (PoC assumption). With fewer than 5 Junioren, all of them go.

## 4. Competitions

### 4.1 Order and scoring

- PoC assumption: the weather (d6, §8: 1 Regen, 2 Hitze, 3 Wind, 4 Kälte, 5–6 Normal) is rolled and
  shown **before** trainer choice and lineup, so the trainer is the reaction to the weather.
  Ergo-Cups have no weather.
- Each player picks one trainer for the whole competition (§7.1); required as soon as the player
  enters at least one race.
- Score per entry (§10.1 working model, PoC weights):
  `crew = Σ over rowers Σ weight·stat` (rounded at the end),
  `total = crew + boat value + (trainer effect + trainer mood) × seats + event bonuses − Krebs penalty`
  (the boat value counts once per entry, `rules.json` `boatValuePerSeat: false`, `boatValueFactor: 1`).
- PoC assumption: per competition each player enters **at most one crew per race**; every rower
  starts **at most once** per competition and every boat is used **at most once** per competition.
  Races may be left empty.
- PoC assumption: in crew boats (2x, 4x) of the A and B categories, rowers of the next younger age
  group may row up (A races accept B, B races accept Kinder). Singles (1x) and ergos are strictly by
  age. This keeps crew races fillable with 3 rowers per age group.
- Ties are kept (§1). Ranking uses competition ranking (1, 1, 3): two golds → the next is third,
  no silver (PoC). A tie for second gives two silvers.
- One medal per **race entry**, not per crew member. Gold = 2 points, silver = 1 point (§1).
- **Siegesmotivation** (designer rule, replaces the permanent Gold motivation of §1; `rules.json`
  `winMotivation`): every winning placement – **Gold or Silber** (1st and 2nd place; there is no
  bronze) – gives every rower of that crew a temporary **+1 Motivation**:
  - It is only ever one point per rower. Winning again while it is active does not stack; it
    resets the duration (refresh).
  - It counts in the next **3 competitions** and then expires. Every scored competition of the
    calendar counts – water regattas, Hildesheim, Ergo-Cups and JTFO alike – whether or not the
    rower starts in it. Implementation: after each competition the engine first counts every
    active bonus down by one, then grants the new wins (so a win at competition _n_ counts at
    _n_+1, _n_+2 and _n_+3).
  - While active it counts in scoring exactly like Motivation (weight × 1), in the guest-free
    lineup estimates (`EstimateEntry`, bots, the UI preview) and in the views (`stats.motivation`
    includes it; `winMotivation` / `winMotivationLeft` show it, e.g. "Motivation 14 (+1 Sieg, noch
    2 Regatten)").
  - It is not a stat change: training, camps, Tausch and stat-based events (Phönix, Verdopplung,
    Seelenzwilling, Wachstum) never see or move it. A crew that capsizes or breaks gets no medal
    and no bonus. At JTFO the team medal gives it to every rower of the team.
- Loan boats (Leihboote, value 0, `rules.json` `loanBoatValue`): in races marked `loanBoat`
  (Hildesheim and JTFO) an entry may leave out its own boat and row a loan boat instead. A loan boat
  is not an own boat, so the one-start-per-boat rule doesn't limit it. Without loan boats a player
  with only the city 1x and 2x could enter just 2 of Hildesheim's 8 races.
- Empty races award nothing. A single finishing starter still has to beat the guest crew (4.6).

### 4.2 Incidents (water only, §10.2)

- PoC assumption (designer rule): one d1000 per boat entry, mutually exclusive, probabilities in
  per mille (`rules.json` `incidents`). Capsizing gets rarer the more rowers a boat has:

  | Boat | Kentern | Materialschaden | Krebs base | Krebs boat size |
  | ---- | ------: | --------------: | ---------: | --------------: |
  | 1x   |     2 % |             3 % |      7.5 % |          × 1.00 |
  | 2x   |     1 % |             3 % |      7.5 % |          × 1.15 |
  | 4x   |   0.5 % |             3 % |      7.5 % |          × 1.30 |
  | 8x   |   0.2 % |             3 % |      7.5 % |          × 1.50 |

  Krebs is scaled per crew (`rules.json` `krebsScaling`): base (or an Ereignis base) × boat-size
  factor × (14 × seats) / crew Technik sum, clamped to 1 %…22.5 %. A crew with more Technik than
  the reference 14 per seat catches fewer crabs.

  Order on the die: Kentern first, then Materialschaden, then Krebs.

- Kentern: the crew finishes last (several capsized crews share the place after all finishers) and
  gets no medal. Materialschaden: DNF (place 0), no medal. Krebs: −10 points.
- No incidents at Ergo-Cups.

### 4.4 JTFO (§11.4)

- PoC assumption: variant by player count: 2 → C (2x + 4x + 4x), 3 → B (2x + 4x + 2x), 4 → A
  (2x + 2x + 2x). All ages and genders allowed.
- Each boat is scored and gets its own incident roll. Team score = sum of the boats; a capsized or
  broken boat contributes 0. One medal per team (gold/silver), Siegesmotivation for all team members.
  Missing own boats are replaced by Leihboote (value 0).

### 4.5 RVB-Endspurtregatta (§11.6)

- Mixed rule (PoC): at least one girl, one boy and two age groups per boat.
- Mixed-Vierer uses an own 4x; the Mixed-Achter uses the best own eight (Kraftwerk 8 or Paintrain 12).

### 4.6 Gastmannschaften (guest crews)

PoC balancing rule (section 10). With at most one crew per player, a race has only 1–3 player
entries and nearly every starter used to medal. Every race with at least one player entry therefore
also has a **guest crew** from another club (`rules.json` `guests`):

- Every stat of a guest rower = `statLevel[oldest allowed age group] + growthPerStation × station
index + guestOffset of the competition` (Kinder 14, B 18, A 22, growth 0.2 per station, so +10
  by the RVB-Endspurt; before the balancing pass 10.3: 15 / 19 / 23 and 0.3).
- Guest total = `round(seats × Σ weights × level)` + guest boat value 5 (scaled like player boats,
  i.e. counted once; no boat at Ergo-Cups, the Kraftwerk value 8 in the eight). No trainer, no incidents.
- The guest crew is ranked like a player entry: if it is faster it takes gold (or silver) away,
  but it never scores points. At JTFO one guest team (sum of the guest boats) joins the team
  ranking only.
- `guestOffset` per competition evens out how hard the guests are (the bots' training concentrates
  on the stats of the next competitions, Kraft above all before the Ergo-Cups): Hildesheim −4.5,
  Mindoor-Cup +1, DRC-Ergo-Cup +5.5, Osnabrücker Ergo-Cup +7, Bremen −1, Kasselregatta −1.5,
  Verbandsregatta I +2.5, Schillerschule +1, Verbandsregatta II 0, JTFO −1.25, RVH −3.5,
  RVB −3.5. They are tuned so that the guest crew wins about 28 % of the contested races of every
  competition in 3-player all-bot games (section 10.3).
- Guest scores are deterministic, so players (and bots) can compute them in advance.

## 5. Events (§12)

  player), each limited by the player's rowers without an event.
- The rower is drawn uniformly among own rowers without an event card; the
  type is drawn from a weighted deck (`events.json`).
- **Scope** (`scope`: `water`, `ergo` or `any`): a card whose bonus only works at water regattas,
  at Ergo-Cups or in a context (sprint) is only dealt while such a competition is still ahead, so
  nobody gets Ergo-Bestzeit-Fieber after the Osnabrücker Ergo-Cup. Water cards may be dealt in the
  winter: they stay on the rower and pay off from Bremen on.
- Entry bonuses (`bonus`) count once per rower with the card in the boat or on the ergometer, at
  every competition that matches the card's scope, context and weathers.

## 6. Ereignisse (§13)

PoC assumption: one card per Ereignis station, drawn with replacement (all weights equal), shown to
everyone. Immediate effects apply at once; the others wait until the next matching station.

**Season-aware deck** (designer rule). Every card has a `scope` (`water`, `ergo` or `any`):

- A `water` card is only drawn when the **next competition** is a water regatta (incl. JTFO), an
  `ergo` card only when it is an Ergo-Cup. In the Ergo-Cup winter (the Ereignisse before
  Mindoor-Cup, DRC-Ergo-Cup and Osnabrücker Ergo-Cup) there is therefore no Maschsee-Monster,
  Hitzewelle or Treibgut; from the Ereignis before Bremen on there are no ergo cards.
- A competition modifier is consumed by the next competition of its scope, which with this rule is
  always the next competition. `any` modifiers (Gastmannschaften) apply at the next competition of
  any kind.
- A modifier that no later station would consume is not drawn either (no Trainerstreik after the
  last training, no Sponsor before the RVB-Endspurt). The deck always has Ruhige Woche as a
  fallback. Pending modifiers without a station left are still hidden from the active list.
- Data rules: Krebs, Materialschaden, weather and city-boat cards must have scope `water`.

| Ereignis                          | Scope | Effect                                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

## 7. Draft (new cards during the season)

- PoC assumption: after every competition except the last, the players pick cards one after another
  from an open offer of (players + 1) cards drawn from the pool. The offer contains at least one boat
  while boats remain; trainers are offered from section 5 on.
- Pick order: fewest medal points first, then fewer golds, then seat order (catch-up).
- Unpicked cards return to the pool.

## 8. Season end

- After the RVB-Endspurtregatta. Points = 2 × Gold + Silber + mission points; most points win, ties
  share the win (PoC).

## 9. Computer version details

- All randomness comes from one seeded generator drawn in a fixed order; a game is fully defined by
  its seed and decision log.
- Draw order per station: entering a new section first draws the trainer moods (one draw per
  trainer, catalog order), then the station draws: event stations per card round and player the
  rower then the type (then mission or tie draws), Ereignis stations the card (then one rower per
  player for Grippewelle-like cards), competitions the weather (d6, unless forced) and at
  resolution one d1000 per water entry in race and player order.
- Opponents' lineups and trainer choices stay hidden until the competition is resolved; all cards on
  the table (rowers, boats, trainers, event cards) are public, mission texts excepted.

### 10.3 Weaker guest crews, a less perfect Taktiker, Siegesmotivation

Designer feedback after playing against the two bots: the guest crews won too often, and the
Taktiker plays like an optimiser. Since a human plays worse than the Taktiker, the guests are tuned
against the bots to win about 25–30 % of the contested races (before: 44 %), evenly across
competition types. Changes:

1. Guest crews (4.6): `statLevel` 15/19/23 → 14/18/22, `growthPerStation` 0.3 → 0.2 (the late
   guests were the strongest: RVH and RVB won 55 %), and every `guestOffset` retuned. JTFO gets an
   offset for the first time (−1.25): its guest team, the sum of three A-level guest boats, won
   95 % of the team rankings. The `sim` tool now reports JTFO team rankings and a guest line per
   competition type.
2. Siegesmotivation (4.1) replaces the permanent Gold motivation.
3. Taktiker bot: perception noise 2 % → 6 % (Bauchgefühl stays at 7 %); the lineup search is
   unchanged. Fewer local-search passes made almost no difference in the sim.
4. Scaling: weaker guests leave more medals to the clubs. With guests alone at ~28 %, the 3-player
   mean rose from 38.4 to 44.7 points. To stay inside 15–40, the five regular regattas have 4
   races instead of 6 (4.3; their race lists are open in the Regelwerk, §11.3): 10 fewer races
   per season, every class still rowed in at least one regular regatta, and less lineup work per
   regatta for a human. Medal points (§1) and the Regelwerk's race lists (Hildesheim, Ergo-Cups)
   are unchanged. A tried alternative – silver only with at least 3 finishing crews (the
   Regelwerk's open point on medals in small fields) together with 5 races per regatta – still
   left 40.3 points.
